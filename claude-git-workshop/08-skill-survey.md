## Scoring rubric (for the organizer)

Use the answers to Q3 and Q4 to assign a level:

| Level | Signal |
|---|---|
| **Beginner** | Q3 = "never used Claude Code" or "Desktop app a few times"; Q4 has only "ChatGPT/Claude.ai" or "None" |
| **Mid** | Q3 = "tried a few times" or "use regularly"; Q4 has 2+ tools but no "shipped to production" |
| **Advanced** | Q3 = "use daily, shipped real work"; OR Q4 includes "built a custom slash command/subagent/MCP" or "shipped to production" |

---

## Pod assembly logic

For 40 participants in 10 pods of 4:

**Target mix per pod:** 1 advanced + 2 mid + 1 beginner.

**Adjustments:**
- If you have fewer than 10 advanced, double up the strongest advanced in the most beginner-heavy pods.
- If you have more than 10 advanced, put 2 advanced in pods where the topic of Part 3 (PRD round-trip with subagents) would benefit most.
- Try to keep PMs and designers mixed within each pod — Part 2 (Figma) is much better with at least one designer at every table.
