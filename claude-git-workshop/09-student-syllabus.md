## How the day works

You'll sit in a pod of 4 — one experienced person, two mid-level, one beginner — pre-assigned by the skill survey. Each Part follows the same shape: instructor demo (~15 min) → independent pod work (~25 min) → 2–3 pods share back.

**Five rules for the pod:**

1. Rotate the keyboard — different person types each Part.
2. Beginner reads Claude's output aloud — that's how the room catches Claude when it's wrong.
3. The experienced person is the **Challenger** — drive the pushback, not the keyboard.
4. Push back on Claude using the prompts on the cheat sheet.
5. Ask your pod before you ask the instructor — pedagogy happens at the table.

## What this workshop is NOT teaching

So you can plan accordingly:

- ❌ AI ethics, bias, hallucination management → important, but not 4-hour content
- ❌ AI evals & observability for production AI features → a specialty in itself
- ❌ Multi-tool coverage (ChatGPT + Gemini + Cursor + n8n) → we go deep on one tool
