# Gambit conversation

A conversation explores possible basketball deals and roster scenarios. Several alternatives can remain relevant at once.

**Proposal version**: A particular arrangement of assets, destinations and hypothetical assumptions, together with its checks. An edited version retains its relationship to the version it came from.
_Avoid_: Current trade, active trade

**Hypothetical scenario**: A set of assumed contracts or signings for specified teams. Its assumptions apply to that scenario and its descendants, rather than changing the actual roster.

**Search version**: A particular search goal and scope, including its fixed assets, replacement slots, constraints and exclusions. Changing its scope creates another version.

**Result batch**: An ordered group of proposals shown together as the result of a search. References such as “the middle option” concern a particular batch.

**Pending question**: An unresolved question associated with a particular request. Discussing something else can hide its choices while leaving the original request available.

**Discussion reference**: A proposal, search, result batch, question or outcome relevant to what the user is discussing. A conversation can discuss several records together.
