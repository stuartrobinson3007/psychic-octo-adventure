# Trade-machine features in chat

These features extend named two- to four-team trades. Contract edits and signings are hypothetical proposal settings; the upstream roster is never rewritten. Every checked edit records a new immutable revision. Follow-ups, undo, explanations, saved cards and conversation reloads retain the settings and asset routes.

| Ask in chat                                                                            | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “What if Derrick White's annual salary were $18 million?”                              | Overrides salary for the focused trade and obtains a fresh engine verdict.                                                                                                                    |
| “Give White two remaining years, keeping that salary.”                                 | Changes duration while retaining the salary and every other asset.                                                                                                                            |
| “Reset White's contract.”                                                              | Restores original contract values.                                                                                                                                                            |
| “Show Boston's available trade exceptions.”                                            | Lists current exceptions with amounts, source names and expiration dates.                                                                                                                     |
| “Use Boston's Anfernee Simons trade exception.”                                        | Selects that listed, unexpired exception for Boston and rechecks the trade. Multiple unidentified choices prompt a question.                                                                  |
| “What contract options can Boston offer Tre Scott?”                                    | Reads live eligible tiers and salary/duration limits.                                                                                                                                         |
| “Sign Tre Scott to Boston for one year at the minimum.”                                | Checks the exact player/team/offer with the signing service and stages a hypothetical contract. A generic signing asks for terms. An explicitly requested minimum can default to one year.    |
| “Now sign-and-trade Tre Scott to Miami.”                                               | Explains why the one-year Boston signing cannot be immediately traded. A sign-and-trade requires the previous team and a new three- or four-year contract with a fully guaranteed first year. |
| “Include Aaron Holiday signing for New York at the minimum for one year in that deal.” | Retains the trade and checks a separate signing linked to its exact version. The signing card shows contract terms and payroll using current team data, explicitly excluding post-trade eligibility.                                                 |
| “Add Juhann Begarin's draft rights to Miami.”                                          | Adds the separately identified, zero-salary asset from its authoritative current owner.                                                                                                       |
| “Send those rights to Chicago instead.”                                                | Changes the destination, adding the named team if needed and keeping existing transfers. The four-team limit applies.                                                                         |
| “Stretch and waive Sam Hauser.”                                                        | Computes the annual obligation as salary × remaining years ÷ (2 × remaining years + 1), rounded to dollars. The waived player cannot be traded.                                               |

Standalone signing or salary/stretch scenarios can contain one to four explicitly identified teams without inventing a trade partner. Their cards say **Hypothetical** and report payroll arithmetic. Signing contracts are checked individually against the current team cap data; combined signing eligibility and trade salary matching are not checked for a standalone scenario. Stretch results are a payroll what-if, not a complete assessment of waiver timing or every CBA restriction.

Trades send `salaryOverrides`, `signedFreeAgents`, selected exception IDs and draft-right IDs to bball-GM's validator. Local checks enforce known identities, current asset ownership, signing status, valid contract ranges and exception ownership/expiry. Returned team IDs, salary-in/out and final payroll evidence must match the exact proposal. Service failures remain “Unavailable”, never a passing trade. Remaining-years and stretch flags are local scenario metadata.

A direct signing joins its signing team. A sign-and-trade transfers a newly signed player to another team. Local sign-and-trade checks enforce three- or four-year terms, the previous-team association, specified first-year guarantees, minimum-exception duration and prohibited signing exceptions and the receiving team’s first-apron limit. The adapter omits the provider’s sign-and-trade flag: live checks found that it incorrectly rejected salary returning to a sender whose final payroll was below the first apron. Salary matching still uses the provider’s signed-player evidence. Eligible full Bird-rights and cap-room routes allow checked amounts up to their ceilings; their funding labels survive alongside the service’s separate contract-price classification.

Ordinary signings are separate proposals. `relatedTrade` links a signing to an exact trade version without inheriting its assumptions or claiming joint approval. The tool rejects combining an ordinary signing with trade transfers; a genuine sign-and-trade retains the signed player and transfer together. Replacement searches modify the trade only, preserving the separately recorded signing.

Sign-and-trades show **Checks incomplete** when the available salary checks pass but previous-season roster history, timing, prior salary or bonuses cannot be verified. The card names the missing checks; it does not ask the user to review or manually validate the deal. Confirmed rule violations show **Invalid**. Older combined signing records remain preserved with truthful limits.

Browser/review imports discard client verdicts, deduplicate proposal references and automatically check every unique proposal with bounded concurrency. Missing checks in older server conversations are repaired automatically on load. Checked historical versions remain immutable, and matching saved cards receive the same fresh evidence. Only an actual service failure shows **Retry check**; imports do not create a manual checking workflow.

Custom pick protections, swaps, automatic pick/right discovery and automatic multi-team discovery retain their existing limits. The features here extend explicit packages and do not introduce basketball-value ratings or simulated acceptance by another GM.

## Verification

Run `node scripts/check-signing-transactions.mjs /tmp/gambit-signing-transactions.json` for live signing contracts, separately linked signing proposals, legacy combined records, sign-and-trade restrictions, receiving-team hard caps and conversational undo. `--mechanics-only` skips model conversations. The UX review includes direct signings, retained signings during replacement searches, rejected short contracts and sign-and-trades with returning players. These checks use real services; no new unit tests are required.

Run `node scripts/check-trade-machine.mjs /tmp/gambit-trade-machine-live.json` for isolated live conversations against the configured model and bball-GM service. It exercises edits, draft-right rerouting, exception selection/removal, signing options, sign-and-trades, undo, stretch and clarification follow-ups. This uses the configured OpenAI key and consumes model usage.

[Live evidence](evidence/trade-machine-live.json) records prior requests, exact resulting proposals/verdicts and tool names for the retained features. The removed export flow is excluded.

The sign-and-trade restrictions follow Article VII §8(e)(1) of the [2023 NBA Collective Bargaining Agreement](https://imgix.cosmicjs.com/25da5eb0-15eb-11ee-b5b3-fbd321202bdf-Final-2023-NBA-Collective-Bargaining-Agreement-6-28-23.pdf). Contract option years and signing dates are not represented by the signing tool. A user request for those terms must receive an explanation of that limit rather than silently being converted to an ordinary contract.

Run `node scripts/check-conversation-loading.mjs /tmp/gambit-conversation-loading.json` for real-service checks of imported historical proposals, duplicate references, signing links, invalid archived proposals, and automatic repair of missing card evidence. [Separate-signing live conversations](evidence/separate-signing-conversations-live.json) include the compound edit-and-sign request.
